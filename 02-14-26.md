I haven't kept a log in awhile. I haven't worked on the car in a few weeks because it's been so frigid, I didn't want to lose my hands while turning wrenches.

Dad came over yesterday to help me get the motor in. I was able to get the engine into the bay on the hoist. Getting the motor and transmission to mate at the correct angle was another challenge altogether. It took a lot of adjusting the angle of the trans, the angle of the motor, and the position of the motor mount bolts to line up correctly. We were able to get the mounts in, but the alignment was an issue still. After tightening some bolts, it felt off and we stopped after a while.

Today, I removed all the bolts and loosened the nuts and tried to get the angle right so that all the bolts would start easily. After that I threaded some in, working my way around the motor to pull it in evenly. After enough threads, I lowered the transmission and then lowered the motor. It is now off the hoist and in its position. Now, I have to work backwards and make sure I check off everything to get it back where it was. Fingers crossed!
