# 12/29/25

Today, I popped into the garage to hopefully make some progress. And...I did! The goal was to remove the crankshaft bolt out to remove the sprocket to gain access to the oil pump. After removing the crankshaft bits, I was able to remove the oil pump to get access to replace the O-ring, as well as the crankshaft front seal.

The new seals are ready to go and I removed some of the left over sealant residue on the block where the oil pump is mounted. A ton of people on the forums say that you should retorque the backing plate screws. i checked to see if they were loose, and those suckers were on there TIGHT. So, I left those alone.

The water pump is in, but I'm going to get a 1/4" and 3/8" torque wrench for the smaller bolts all over the motor and engine bay. I also need some more sealant to use for the oil pump reinstallation. After that is back on, I can add the water pump, the heads, timing belt, and so on and so on. I'm hoping once that this hump is passed, things will move fairly quickly.
