# 01-04-26

Great day today! I didn't do a large quantity of work, but I did the, quite possibly, the most crucial step done. The heads are on!!!

The process felt a little overwhelming and I checked the service manual probably 100 times until I was sure I had the steps down. I wrote them down and made a checklist for each step.

The steps:
1. Tighten all to 22 ft/lbs
2. Tighten all to 51 ft/lbs
3. Loosen all bolts 180°
4. Repeat step 3
5. Tighten 1 and 2 (center bolts) to 25 ft/lbs
6. Tighten the rest to 11 ft/lbs
7. Tighten all to 90°
8. Repeat step 7

Quite the process, but it's done! Now, I'll just be anxious about it until I turn the key for the first time when it's back in the car.
